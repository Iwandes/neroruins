# Expedition tools

## Expedition Map

Use it to open your **Expedition Record**: the ruins you have entered, newest first, with family, size,
world and the hub position. Only you can see your record. Positions are dropped after the server's
retention period (or never recorded if the server turns position recording off), and then show as
"Position not kept". A ruin counts as entered when you stand inside its hub.

The map needs NeroRuins on your client; otherwise it points you to `/neroruins privacy export`.
Expedition Maps turn up in tier-1 loot.

## Survey Scanner

Use it to ping for ruins. The action bar shows the **family, compass direction and a distance band** of the
nearest ruin hub, for example "Survey Bunker, east, close by", or that there is no signal in range. It never
shows coordinates. It has a ten-second cooldown and searches a bounded area (about 2 km for planet bunkers,
farther for rarer ruins); satellites do not register, only hubs. Survey Scanners turn up in tier-2 loot.

## Surveyor's Compass

The chapter-one relic. While held, it points toward the nearest ruin **you have not entered yet**, based on
your own Expedition Record. See [Vaults and loot](Vaults-and-Loot.md#relics).
