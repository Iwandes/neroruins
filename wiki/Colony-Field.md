# Colony Field

The sprawl of a lost colony: habitats, streets, dead fields, silos, a landing pad and a cemetery. Colony
fields are the least dangerous ruins and the richest in logs.

## Where it generates (requires Nerospace)

| Size | Where |
| --- | --- |
| **Citadel** | Glacira (and its spires and methane shore). Rare. |
| Complex | Greenxertz (and its spore marsh and fungal depths) |
| Outpost | Mars, Glacira and Greenxertz |

## The citadel

A landing pad with a wreck opens onto a street of habitats, a generator, a school, a store and a dead field.
At the end stands the **council hall**: a Ruin Spawner, a **conduit-routing** gate and the council vault.
Behind the vault, a sanctum holds the chapter's last log and a **relic socket** that opens a reliquary.
The council vault always holds the relic the socket asks for. Satellites — hab rings, fields, silos,
a cemetery and wrecks — spread out to roughly 200 blocks.

## Complexes and outposts

- The **complex** has its own chapter of logs and a vault sealed by **Ballast Plates** in a guarded room.
- **Outposts** are small: a keycard in a chest, a card reader, a Sealed Door and a vault.

## Guardians

Mostly Sentinel Mk I; colonies are the gentlest ruins ([Guardians](Guardians.md)).

## Protection

In the citadel the council hall, the passage beyond it, the sanctum and the reliquary are enclosed in
Reinforced Survey Hull; the outpost's vault room too. The complex's plate-sealed vault room is mineable,
like the bunker. Streets, homes and satellites are mineable.

## Not yet included

Cemetery markers carry no names yet, and colony farms use vanilla plants rather than NeroAgriculture
