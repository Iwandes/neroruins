# Crash Site

Wrecked Survey ships, half-buried where they came down, with debris scattered around them.

## Where it generates (requires Nerospace)

| Size | Where |
| --- | --- |
| **The *Meridian* (citadel)** | Cindara. A Survey heavy freighter. Rare. |
| Complex (a hauler) | every moon and planet |
| Outpost (a pod) | every moon and planet |

## The *Meridian*

You enter through a hull breach into the breach hall, then the cargo hold, the crew deck and a guardian
approach with two spawners. Solving the cargo hold's **conduit loop** opens a crew locker that always holds
the **Meridian Keystone** — the relic the cargo-bay **relic socket** asks for. Past the socket are the inner
bay and the cockpit vault. The chapter's logs run along the route in order. A **debris field** of plates,
containers, escape pods and engine parts with salvage spreads out to about 300 blocks.

## Haulers and pods

- **Haulers** have a hold, a guarded conduit gate and a cockpit vault, with a smaller debris field.
- **Pods** have a spawner, a chest with a Survey Keycard, a card reader gate and a vault.

## Protection

The hull is sunk into the ground; the cockpit, inner bay, keystone locker and pod vault rooms are enclosed in
Reinforced Survey Hull. The rest of the hull is mineable.
