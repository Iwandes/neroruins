# Lattice Temple

The precincts of the Architects, built from Latticework, Lattice Floor and Glyph Stone and lit by
Resonance Crystal.

## Where it generates (requires Nerospace)

| Size | Where |
| --- | --- |
| **The Lattice Citadel** | Nyxara (dayside, nightside and twilight band). Rare. |
| Complex | Nyxara, and a separate log-free complex on Cindara |
| Wayshrine (outpost) | every moon and planet |

## The Lattice Citadel

A plaza and outer hall lead to two **spiral wells** that wind down to a sanctum about **86 blocks below the
plaza**. On the way down: a guardian hall, a **glyph-sequence gate**, a **resonance organ** and a final
room with a **relic socket**. Side precincts branch off the main route with halls and annexes. Obelisk
fields and other satellites surround the citadel, out to roughly 200 blocks.

The citadel tells the last chapter of the story; its logs follow the route in order, with the final
entries deepest.

## Complexes and wayshrines

- **Complexes** have a guardian hall, a glyph gate and a vault. The Nyxara complex holds that chapter's logs,
  and its vault always contains the relic that the citadel's socket asks for, so the citadel is never a dead
  end.
- **Wayshrines** are small: a single guarded door, a glyph gate and a sealed vault.

## Puzzles and guardians

Glyph sequences, resonance tuning and a relic socket ([Puzzles](Puzzles.md)). Ruin Spawners of rising tier
guard the halls before each gate, with Arc Grates in places ([Guardians](Guardians.md)).

## Protection

Every room behind a gate — gate vaults, the organ, the final room and the sanctum — is enclosed in
Reinforced Latticework and Reinforced Lattice Floor, which cannot be mined or blown up. Halls and stairs
outside the gates are mineable. There are no windows into gated rooms.
